Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They allow the website to recognise your device on subsequent visits and to gather information about how you use the site.

How We Use Cookies

Antarctic Peninsula Cruise Guide uses cookies for one purpose only: analytics. We collect anonymous data about how visitors use this site — which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and which links are clicked — in order to improve the quality and accuracy of our editorial content.

We do not use advertising cookies. We do not place tracking cookies for third-party ad networks. We do not share cookie data with operators listed on this site.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Analytics cookies Anonymous traffic and behaviour data to improve site content Up to 26 months
Session cookies Technical operation of the site (navigation, page loading) Session only (deleted on browser close)

Third-Party Cookies

If we use a third-party analytics service (such as Google Analytics or a privacy-focused alternative), that service may place its own cookies on your device. These analytics cookies collect anonymous, aggregated data only. No personally identifiable information is collected or shared.

How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View all cookies currently stored on your device
  • Delete cookies individually or in bulk
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies (note: this may affect the functionality of some websites)

Instructions for managing cookies are available in the help section of every major browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we change the analytics tools we use or the types of data we collect. Any significant change will be noted in the footer of this site with an updated date.
